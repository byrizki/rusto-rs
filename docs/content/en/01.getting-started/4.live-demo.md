## What You Can Test

::card-group
:::card
---

title: 100% Client-Side WASM
icon: i-lucide-shield-check
---

Every model weight and image pixel stays on your device. Inference runs locally via `@rustors/web` with pluggable WebAssembly engines: `rusto-nn` (default pure-Rust MNN), `rusto-rten`, or `rusto-webgpu` compute shaders.
:::

:::card
---

title: Drop, Paste, or Pick Samples
icon: i-lucide-image-up
---

Test built-in invoices, passports, handwriting, and dense newspapers—or drag-and-drop and paste (`Ctrl+V`) any screenshot or photo from your clipboard.
:::

:::card
---

title: Multi-Format Inspection
icon: i-lucide-table-2
---

Hover bounding boxes to cross-highlight text, switch between `Lines` and `Words`, reconstruct 2D `Spatial` layouts, and export `TSV` (Sheets/Excel) or `JSON`.
:::

:::card
---

title: Instant SDK Code Generation
icon: i-lucide-code-2
---

Tune thresholds, reading order, or descreening presets in **Settings**, then open the **Code** tab to copy the exact `@rustors/web` Fluent Builder snippet.
:::
::
