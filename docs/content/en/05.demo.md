## How It Works

1. **Client-Side Processing**: The OCR inference pipeline executes directly on your machine using WebAssembly.
2. **Interactive Bounding Boxes**: Hover over any detected bounding box on the image canvas to inspect the recognized text and confidence percentage. Click any box to copy its text.
3. **Flexible Granularity**: Switch between line-level grouping (`lines`), individual word tokens (`words`), and spatial ASCII layout reconstruction (`spatial`).
4. **Data Export**: Export results instantly to Clipboard, CSV, or structured JSON.
